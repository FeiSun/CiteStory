1. AtlasA globe that unfolds into a live street-level map. Replay how citations spread year by year, and search any city, country or institution.
  2. ReachThe world measured from home: every city at its true distance and bearing. How far did the ideas travel?
  3. StoryAn illustrated long read: the first citations, the breakout papers, and the spread across continents.
  4. FlowNo map at all: ribbons from each paper to the continents and countries that cite it.
  5. ConstellationEvery paper as a star, ringed by the papers that cite it, one ring per year. Lines join the papers that are cited together.
  6. RaceRank by citations as ribbons through time. Where two ribbons cross, one paper overtakes another.
  7. RegularsThe authors who keep coming back: one row each, one square for every paper of theirs that cites this work.
  8. RecordOne revolution a year, and every citation a cut in the groove. Play it, scratch it, listen.
